Special notes
1. All "MSIE Library" commands work with a web page opened.
2. If you possess a copy of "Internet Explorer" v. 4.5 and you wish work with "MSIE Library", probably you'll need open it and replace all occurrences of "Internet Explorer" with "Internet Explorer 4.5", or rename your copy to "Internet Explorer", without the version.
3. Not tested in OSX, but it should work (perhaps after a re-compilation).
4. "bookmark()" command is very beta. So, be careful and test it with a duplicate of your favorites (into "Explorer" folder, into your Preferences folder), or you could lost all it.
5. If you're working with a big page (greater than 300K or with a lot of images) and you get errors, you should increase YOUR app's memory (script editor or the applet itself).
